The things buyers ask first

Practical answers about how we work, what it costs, and what you own at the end.

We turn stalled GenAI pilots into governed, production-grade conversational AI that your own team can operate. A typical engagement is fixed-scope and runs two weeks: diagnose, deploy inside your existing messaging tools, put monitoring and a compliance loop around it, then transfer ownership to you.

Beehive Strategy Limited is a Hong Kong company. We work with clients across APAC and internationally, and we deliver remotely into the messaging platforms your team already uses, so location is rarely a constraint on delivery.

A pilot proves a model can answer a question. A governed deployment proves it can keep answering correctly next quarter, under changed rules, with a named owner and an audit trail. We build the second thing, and we hand it over rather than operate it for you indefinitely.

Two weeks is realistic because the scope is fixed before we start and the engine behind the work is already built. Ninety-five automated checks have to pass before any release goes live, which is what keeps a short timeline from becoming a quality gamble.

WeChat Work, DingTalk, Feishu, WhatsApp, Microsoft Teams, Telegram, LINE and your own website. The point is that your team does not adopt a new tool — the assistant comes to where they already work.
