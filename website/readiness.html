Free audit
Free · 24 questions · no sales call

How ready is your business for AI that actually runs?

Twenty-four questions across six axes, in about six minutes. You get one radar profile showing where you are solid and where AI would break — and what to fix first. It runs in your browser; nothing is sent to us unless you choose to email yourself a copy.

Self-assessment

Twenty-four questions, six axes

Answer honestly — the value is in the gaps, not the score. Pick the level that is true today, not the one you plan to reach.

1 / 6

Axis 01

Data & grounding

Can an assistant answer from governed data, or would it be guessing?

01 Is there one agreed source of truth for the numbers your team reports?

02 Can the data an assistant would answer from be reached through an API or governed layer — not a manual export?

03 Do you know how fresh that data is, and where it goes stale?

04 Are key definitions (revenue, active customer, margin) documented and used consistently across teams?

Axis 02

Governance & accountability

When an answer is wrong, who is accountable — and is that written down?

05 Is a named person accountable for whether an AI-generated answer is correct?

06 Are the business rules the AI must follow written down, rather than held by one person?

07 Is there a defined escalation path when an output looks wrong?

08 Do those rules have a scheduled review, or are they set once and forgotten?

Axis 03

Access & permissions

Would the assistant show the right numbers to the right people — and only them?

09 Is access to data defined by role rather than by individual?

10 Can you enforce least privilege — so a finance analyst cannot see HR data?

11 Is there a record of who queried what?

12 Would an assistant inherit your existing permission model rather than create a new one?

Axis 04

Channels & adoption

Will people actually use it, where they already work?

13 Does your team already work mainly inside a messaging tool — Teams, WhatsApp, WeChat Work, DingTalk or Feishu?

14 Would a new dashboard or portal realistically be opened every day?

15 Are the people who would use this involved in defining it?

16 Is there an internal owner who will champion adoption?

Axis 05

Monitoring & compliance

Would you notice if it started drifting?

17 Would you notice if the AI's answers began to drift from the rules?

18 Is there an audit trail of what was asked and what was answered?

19 Can you measure whether it is being used — and by whom?

20 Is there a scheduled review of outputs against the agreed rules?

Axis 06

Ownership & capability

Could your team run this after the vendor leaves?

21 Does your team have the skills to operate and adjust it?

22 Is there documentation your team would own and maintain?

23 Have you agreed what happens on the day the engagement ends?

24 Is there executive sponsorship and budget to keep it running past the pilot?
