Beehive launches a free AI readiness self-assessment

Beehive Strategy today launched a free AI readiness self-assessment, available in English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese.

The assessment asks 24 questions across six axes: data and grounding, governance and accountability, access and permissions, channel adoption, monitoring and compliance, and ownership and capability.

What you get

  • A radar profile of your organisation across all six axes
  • An overall readiness score from 0 to 100
  • The weakest axis named, with a suggested fix-first action

The score and radar are shown instantly, with no email required. Visitors who want a full written profile can optionally leave a work email and receive a personalised report.

The tool runs in the browser and is free to use at beehivestrategy.com/readiness.
