Delivery method

The same four steps, either route

Whether you arrive through training or a project, the discipline is identical: diagnose, deploy, govern, hand over.

01

Diagnose

A 24-question self-audit across six axes gives you a radar profile before any commitment — or, for projects, a scoping conversation anchored on the same six axes.

02

Deploy in 2 weeks

For the core engagement: fixed scope, governed build, change control and audit logging in place before go-live. For training: the cohort is scheduled and materials are tailored to your systems.

03

Govern & hand over

Monitoring, alerting and a compliance loop, then ownership transfer. In training, the handover is the vocabulary your team keeps using after we leave.

04

Retainer (optional)

Keep it evolving: monitoring, flow iteration, compliance re-checks and periodic impact reporting. First 90 days included in the project price.

What you avoid

Three costs that never appear on a quote

The expensive part of AI is rarely the build. It is what happens after launch, when nobody is watching.

Silent drift

Quality degrades for weeks before anyone notices, because there is no baseline to drift from.

Compliance exposure

Rules change and your AI keeps answering with yesterday's logic. The exposure is yours, not the vendor's.

Rebuild on exit

If you do not own the configuration, leaving means starting again from zero.

Standards

The same standard on both routes

Training teaches the standard. Deployment implements it. Neither is optional.

Auditability

If it cannot be audited, it is not production.

Governance is what separates a demo from a system you can leave running unsupervised — and what lets your team take ownership without inheriting risk they cannot see.

  • Change control Defined permissions with a full audit log behind every change.
  • Scheduled compliance review Cross-border tax rules for five tiers are coded into a tool in daily use and re-checked on schedule.
  • Documented handover Built into the project scope, never sold as an extra.
