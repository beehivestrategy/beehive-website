# About Beehive Strategy

## Company Overview

**Beehive Strategy Limited** (深圳蜂启咨询有限公司) is an enterprise AI and data analytics consulting firm headquartered in Shenzhen, Guangdong, China. Founded in 2024, the company specialises in MCP-powered conversational BI platforms, AI strategy consulting, and enterprise data governance.

## Core Expertise

Beehive Strategy operates at the intersection of three technology domains:

1. **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** — Designing and deploying MCP servers that connect AI assistants to enterprise data sources through a standardised protocol
2. **Conversational BI** — Building natural-language analytics platforms that let business users query data without SQL, deployed inside WeChat Work, DingTalk, Feishu, WhatsApp, Slack, Microsoft Teams, and Telegram
3. **Enterprise AI Governance** — Implementing AI risk frameworks, model monitoring, compliance automation, and data governance programs
