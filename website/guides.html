Method

How these guides are built

Research-first, not opinion

Every guide starts from how the best enterprise teams actually deploy AI — then we strip it down to the decisions you have to make, not the buzzwords.

Built to be used in a meeting

Each one ends with a checklist or a starting point you can paste into a planning doc. They are written to be acted on this week, not filed away.

Maintained with the product

When our deployment method changes, the guides change. We do not ship a PDF and forget it.

No thought-leadership filler

If a section does not help you decide or deploy, it is not in the guide. You get the working method, nothing padded.
