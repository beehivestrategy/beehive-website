# Industry Solutions — Beehive Strategy

Beehive Strategy delivers specialised MCP-powered conversational BI solutions across five industry verticals. Each solution includes pre-built AI agents, domain-specific data models, and industry KPI frameworks.

## Retail & E-Commerce

Real-time sales insights, inventory optimisation, and AI-driven demand forecasting for retail and e-commerce businesses.

**Key Capabilities:**

- Real-time sales analytics with natural-language querying
- AI-driven demand forecasting and inventory optimisation
- Omnichannel conversion tracking and customer journey analytics
- Dynamic pricing intelligence and promotional ROI measurement
- Customer segmentation and lifetime value prediction

**Pre-built KPIs:** Same-store sales growth, inventory turnover ratio, customer acquisition cost, average order value, cart abandonment rate

## Financial Services

Intelligent risk control, compliance automation, and real-time analytics for banks, insurance companies, and fintech firms.

**Key Capabilities:**

- Real-time risk monitoring with millisecond anomaly detection
- Regulatory compliance automation (Basel III/IV, PIPL, GDPR)
- Credit risk modelling and portfolio analytics
- Anti-money laundering pattern detection
- T+0 real-time risk views for trading operations

**Pre-built KPIs:** Non-performing loan ratio, Value at Risk (VaR), compliance score, operational risk index, customer churn rate

## Manufacturing

Connected production intelligence, predictive maintenance, and supply chain optimisation for manufacturing enterprises.

**Key Capabilities:**

- Real-time OEE (Overall Equipment Effectiveness) tracking
- Predictive maintenance with AI-driven failure prediction
- Quality control analytics and defect root-cause analysis
- Supply chain visibility and logistics route optimisation
- Energy consumption monitoring and sustainability reporting

**Pre-built KPIs:** OEE percentage, mean time between failures, defect rate, throughput per line, energy cost per unit

## Professional Services

Resource allocation, project profitability, and client relationship analytics for consulting, legal, and professional services firms.

**Key Capabilities:**

- Billable hours tracking and utilisation analytics
- Project profitability and margin analysis
- Resource allocation optimisation
- Client relationship health scoring
- Pipeline forecasting and capacity planning

**Pre-built KPIs:** Utilisation rate, average billable rate, project margin, client retention rate, revenue per consultant

## Real Estate

Property valuation, market intelligence, and portfolio analytics for real estate developers and investment firms.

**Key Capabilities:**

- AI-driven property valuation models
- Market trend analysis and competitive positioning
- Portfolio performance tracking and risk assessment
- Development feasibility analysis
- Tenant analytics and occupancy optimisation

**Pre-built KPIs:** Net asset value, occupancy rate, yield on cost, rental growth rate, development pipeline value
