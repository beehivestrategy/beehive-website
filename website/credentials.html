Who we are

Built to Ship AI Into the Tools You Already Use

Beehive Strategy (蜂启咨询) helps mid-market and enterprise teams turn fragmented enterprise data into decisions people can act on — through natural-language analytics that live inside the messaging and collaboration tools they already use.

01 / EMBEDDED

Embedded, not remote

Our Forward-Deployed Engineering model ships AI into your real environment — WeChat Work, DingTalk, Feishu, WhatsApp, Telegram, Teams — instead of a separate dashboard nobody opens.

02 / SPEED

Production in weeks

A working conversational-BI deployment can be live within two weeks of kickoff, then hardened with access control, audit, and monitoring.

03 / GOVERNANCE

Governed by default

Role-based access, full audit trails, and data-residency-aware deployment are part of the baseline — not an enterprise upsell.
