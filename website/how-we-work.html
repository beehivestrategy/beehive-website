How we work

We deploy governed, production-grade conversational AI inside the messaging tools your team already uses — then hand over the keys. You own the system. We make it run.

STEP 01

Diagnose

A free 24-question AI-readiness self-audit across six axes. You get a radar profile and a plain-language read of where you actually stand — before you spend anything.

STEP 02

Deploy in 2 weeks

Fixed scope, production-grade, governed. Conversation flows built, knowledge base loaded, channels connected, write-back wired. You see it working in your own environment, not ours.

STEP 03

Govern & hand over

Monitoring and alerting switched on, compliance baseline established, audit trail live. Then ownership transfer: documentation, admin credentials and a day of training for your team.

STEP 04

Retainer (optional, recommended)

AI is not install-and-forget. Models drift, customer behaviour shifts, product and pricing change, regulations move. The retainer covers monitoring, flow iteration, compliance re-checks and periodic impact reporting.
