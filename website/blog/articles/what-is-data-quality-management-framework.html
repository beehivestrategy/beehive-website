What Is Data Quality Management?

Data quality management is the discipline of measuring, monitoring, improving, and maintaining the quality of an organisation data assets. It encompasses the policies, processes, and technologies that ensure data is accurate, complete, consistent, timely, and valid — the five core dimensions of data quality.

Poor data quality is one of the most expensive and pervasive problems in enterprise analytics. Gartner estimates that organisations lose an average of $12.9 million annually due to poor data quality. For AI and analytics initiatives, bad data in means bad insights out.

The Five Dimensions of Data Quality

  1. Accuracy. Data correctly represents the real-world entities and events it describes.
  2. Completeness. All required data fields are populated; there are no critical gaps.
  3. Consistency. Data values are uniform across different systems and time periods.
  4. Timeliness. Data is available when needed and reflects the current state.
  5. Validity. Data conforms to defined business rules and constraints.

Key Practices

  • Data profiling. Analysing data to discover anomalies, patterns, and quality issues.
  • Data cleansing. Correcting or removing erroneous, duplicate, or incomplete records.
  • Data standardisation. Enforcing consistent formats, units, and naming conventions.
  • Continuous monitoring. Automated dashboards and alerts that track quality metrics over time.
  • Root cause analysis. Identifying and addressing the source of quality issues, not just the symptoms.
