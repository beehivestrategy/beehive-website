Frequently Asked Questions

Questions about Beehive Strategy's industry-specific AI and conversational BI solutions.

Industry-specific conversational BI delivers pre-built AI agents, data models, and analytics templates tailored to each sector's unique KPIs and workflows. We provide specialised solutions for retail, financial services, manufacturing, professional services, and real estate, deployable in weeks via WeChat Work, DingTalk, Feishu, WhatsApp, Microsoft Teams, and Telegram.

Beehive Strategy focuses on five industries: retail and e-commerce, financial services, manufacturing, professional services, and real estate. Each vertical has pre-built AI agents, industry-specific data models, and domain expertise to accelerate deployment.

Conversational BI enables retail teams to ask questions like 'What were our top 10 SKUs by margin last quarter?' and get instant answers in WeChat Work, DingTalk, Feishu, WhatsApp, Microsoft Teams, or Telegram. Beehive Strategy's retail solution includes AI agents for demand forecasting, inventory optimisation, omnichannel conversion tracking, and customer lifetime value prediction.

For financial services, we provide AI-driven risk control automation, real-time regulatory reporting, credit risk modelling, and compliance monitoring. The platform connects to banking core systems and delivers T+0 risk views through conversational queries, with the governing sources shown alongside each answer.

The manufacturing solution focuses on OEE (Overall Equipment Effectiveness) tracking, predictive maintenance, production analytics, and supply chain optimisation. AI agents monitor equipment health in real-time and alert teams before failures occur, with an audit trail for every answer.

Yes. Professional services firms use the platform for resource allocation optimisation, project profitability analysis, utilisation rate tracking, and client billing analytics. The same governed actions can be reused across teams; we do not publish client outcome figures here.

Yes. The real estate solution includes property valuation models, market intelligence analytics, tenant segmentation, and investment risk assessment. AI agents can analyse property market data, rental trends, and comparable transactions to support faster investment decisions.
