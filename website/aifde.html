Delivery Model

Linear Effort or Exponential Value?

The real FDE differentiator is NOT "sending people on-site." It's whether assets accumulate after each deployment. We design for the exponential curve.

Linear Curve

Each project starts from scratch. Same effort, same overhead, no reuse. Margins stay flat. The heavier it gets, the harder it scales. This is traditional outsourcing dressed as FDE.

Exponential Curve

Each deployment produces reusable assets: industry templates, semantic layer patterns, proven agent configurations. Margins improve. The next deployment is faster. This is how we operate.

Asset Accumulation

Industry-specific data models, pre-built AI agents, metric dictionaries, and operational playbooks. Every client deployment enriches the library for the next one. Your team owns the result.

Questions About FDE & IM-Native Delivery

Everything you need to know about how we deliver Forward Deployed Engineering outcomes without the traditional overhead.

Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE) is a delivery model where tech companies embed their own engineers directly inside client organisations to design, deploy, and operate AI systems on-site. Pioneered by Palantir 20 years ago, FDE became the defining AI delivery model of 2026 when Microsoft, OpenAI, Anthropic, and AWS collectively invested $9 billion in six weeks. A Forward Deployed Engineer is the individual who does that work. Beehive delivers the same embedded AI experience through IM-native architecture — no on-site engineer needed.

We embed AI analytics directly into the IM platform your team already uses — WeChat Work, DingTalk, Feishu, WhatsApp, Microsoft Teams, or Telegram. The AI agent lives where your team works, not on an engineer's laptop. This IM-native architecture achieves the same outcome as traditional FDE (embedded AI in your workflow) without the cost, travel, and friction of sending an engineer on-site.

Microsoft's FDE program costs $2.5 billion for 6,000 on-site engineers. Beehive delivers the same embedded AI experience through IM-native architecture at approximately 1/100th the cost. Engagements are quoted per scope, from the Quick Start plan through to Professional and enterprise programmes. No per-engineer-day billing, no travel costs, no overhead.
