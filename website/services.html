WHERE THE EMPHASIS SITS

Transformation with AI as the main focus

Most programmes start with a platform rebuild and hope AI arrives at the end. We invert the order: fix one live AI outcome first, then build only the data and integration layers it actually needs.

AI in production

Assistants, agents, evaluation, monitoring and the governance loop. This is what we are accountable for.

Data foundation

Semantic definitions, connectors, quality checks and lineage — scoped to the use case, not a greenfield platform.

Digital plumbing

Identity, system integration and channel endpoints. We work around your existing stack, not replace it.

THE METHOD

Two weeks from scope to handover

Diagnose, build, deploy, hand over. Four fixed steps, one named owner on your side, and a written definition of what “working” means before anyone writes code.
