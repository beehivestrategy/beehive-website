What training prevents

Three expensive mistakes it avoids

Seeing these three clearly is what a short course is for.

Committing to the wrong thing

Without evaluation criteria, the loudest vendor wins rather than the right one.

Stalled adoption

Teams resist what they were never given a reason to trust.

No internal owner

If nobody can operate it, the purchase was a donation.

Then deployment

Training is the starting point, not the whole house

Understanding is necessary. It is not the same as having governed AI in production.

The handover

Training ends with a team that can run it.

The point of training is not to sell you a course. It is to make the deployment that follows something your team understands well enough to own.

  • Fixed scope Scope and deliverables agreed before work starts.
  • Governance baseline Who can change what, every change logged.
  • Ownership transfer Code, configuration and documentation are yours.
