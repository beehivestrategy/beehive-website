What Is Industry-Specific Conversational BI?

Industry-specific conversational BI is the practice of tailoring natural-language analytics to the unique KPIs, data sources, and decision workflows of a given sector. Unlike generic BI tools that require every user to learn a one-size-fits-all dashboard, Beehive Strategy pre-maps industry terminology, regulatory frameworks, and common metric hierarchies so that a retail manager asking "What were our top-performing SKUs last weekend?" and a compliance officer asking "Show me any transactions flagged for AML review this quarter" both receive precise, context-aware answers without writing a single line of SQL.
