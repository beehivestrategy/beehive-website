Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting started with Beehive Strategy.

What is conversational BI?
Conversational BI lets users ask business questions in natural language and receive instant data answers, charts, and explanations — without writing SQL or building dashboards.
How long does it take to deploy Beehive Strategy?
Most enterprise customers are live within two weeks. Our implementation team handles data source integration, semantic-layer setup, and user onboarding.
Which data sources can Beehive connect to?
Beehive connects to 50+ sources including SAP, Oracle, Salesforce, SQL databases, data warehouses, REST APIs, and spreadsheets — all through a unified semantic layer.
Is Beehive Strategy secure and compliant?
Yes. Beehive is SOC 2 compliant, GDPR ready, and supports SSO, row-level security, and audit logging. Your raw data stays within your infrastructure.
Can users access Beehive from WeChat Work or DingTalk?
Absolutely. Beehive integrates with WeChat Work, DingTalk, Feishu, web, and mobile so teams can ask questions wherever they already communicate.
