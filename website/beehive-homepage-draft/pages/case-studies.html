Global Manufacturing Enterprise

The Challenge

A multinational manufacturer with 18 production facilities faced frequent unplanned equipment downtime costing millions per quarter. Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our case studies and client engagements.

How do you measure and verify the results shown in case studies?
All metrics are measured through a structured before-and-after analysis conducted jointly with each client. We establish baseline performance indicators during the discovery phase, then track improvements over defined measurement periods using the client's own data systems. Results are verified by both the client's internal analytics team and our customer success team, and all published metrics have been reviewed and approved by the client before publication.
What is the typical timeline from engagement to measurable results?
Most clients achieve their first measurable value within 2 weeks of deployment. Initial use cases are selected during a focused discovery sprint (typically 1 week) to ensure rapid time-to-value. Comprehensive results, such as the metrics shown in our case studies, typically emerge within 3 to 6 months as teams adopt the platform across departments and use cases expand. Our customer success team provides ongoing support to accelerate adoption and ROI realization.
Can Beehive Strategy integrate with our existing tech stack?
Yes. Beehive Strategy connects to 50+ data sources out of the box, including major ERP, CRM, database, and SaaS platforms. Our semantic layer normalizes data across systems to ensure consistent metrics and definitions. For systems not in our standard connector library, we offer custom integration services. The platform is designed to work alongside your existing BI tools, not replace them — complementing tools like Tableau, Power BI, and Looker with natural language accessibility.
Do you offer industry-specific solutions or only generic BI capabilities?
We deliver both. The core Beehive Strategy platform provides a universal conversational BI capability, and we layer industry-specific configurations on top. This includes pre-built semantic models for common industry KPIs (e.g., OEE for manufacturing, NIM for banking, utilization for professional services), industry-specific report templates, and domain-trained AI models that understand industry terminology. This approach lets each client benefit from shared platform improvements while maintaining configurations tailored to their sector.
