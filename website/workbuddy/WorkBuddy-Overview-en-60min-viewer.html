PART 01 / 08
Product definitionEXHIBIT 11.44 min

A workbench that finishes the job

1. Natural-language understanding

No commands to learn, no syntax to memorise. just describe what you need in everyday language — the bar is low enough that no training is needed.

2. Autonomous planning and execution

Complex tasks are broken into steps automatically, tools are called in sequence, and it self-checks and corrects along the way until the goal is met.

3. Multimodal task handling

Understands and generates text, images, tables and code; it parses documents and charts, and produces multimodal output.

4. Local file operations

Within authorised directories, it reads and writes files directly — tidying material, generating documents, batch processing — with output saved straight to disk.

In one line

Tencent's all-scenario AI work platform, covering everyday office work, software development and design — you issue a task in natural language; it thinks, decomposes and plans, then delivers a result you can sign off.

WHY IT MATTERSWhat you get is a finished item that can go straight into the process — not an answer you still have to tidy up.

Personal editionEXHIBIT 291.22 min

Personal: a full toolset for one person

What it solves

One person carrying too much, too varied, too repetitive work — with no team to share it with. The personal edition gives that person an assistant that can act.

Typical users Founders · independent consultants · freelancers · small-team leads · efficiency-minded individuals

What you get

1. 100+ domain experts: no prompt-writing required
2. All core capabilities: planning, execution, multimodal, local files
3. Multiple forms: desktop client / CLI / plugin
4. Cloud hosting: keeps running after you close the client

In one line

What used to take hiring an assistant now takes one person and one subscription.

What it does not manage

No permissions, no audit, no budget, no shared assets — because with one person, none of that is a problem yet.

WHY IT MATTERSThe personal edition solves "I cannot finish this alone" — it does not talk about governance, because you do not need it yet.
