# Consulting Services — Beehive Strategy

Beehive Strategy delivers enterprise consulting through four service pathways, backed by a proven MCP-powered conversational BI platform. Every engagement begins with a clear business outcome and ends with a working system.

## Pathway 01: Enterprise Data Enablement

Modern data foundations for organisations building from the ground up or modernising legacy systems.

**What You Get:**

- **Reference Architecture** — Blueprints for your data platform from ingestion to serving layer, designed for your specific scale and compliance needs
- **Automated Data Pipelines** — Repeatable ingestion patterns for batch and streaming data with built-in quality checks and lineage tracking
- **Governed Semantic Layer** — A business-friendly metrics layer ensuring every team sees the same numbers, powered by the MCP semantic model
- **Governance Model** — RBAC permissions, data classification, audit trails, and stewardship workflows embedded from day one

**Methodology:** Assess & Discover → Architect & Design → Iterative Delivery

## Pathway 02: Advanced Analytics Enablement

From ad-hoc reporting to governed self-serve analytics for organisations ready to scale data-driven decision-making.

**What You Get:**

- **KPI Hierarchy & Metric Dictionary** — A governed catalog of every business metric with clear definitions, owners, and calculation logic
- **Dashboarding Frameworks** — Standardised templates for executive, operational, and team-level dashboards with automated refresh schedules
- **Conversational BI Deployment** — Deploy the MCP-powered platform to WeChat Work, DingTalk, Feishu, WhatsApp, Microsoft Teams, or Telegram so teams can ask data questions in natural language
- **Team Training & Enablement** — Workshops, documentation, and hands-on sessions for confident self-service analytics

**Methodology:** Strategic Alignment → Standardisation & Governance → Scale & Enable

## Pathway 03: Strategic Transformation

Operating model redesign and change management for enterprises embedding AI into core business processes.

**What You Get:**

- **AI Strategy & Roadmap** — A prioritised portfolio of AI use cases aligned to business outcomes with clear investment phasing
- **Operating Model Design** — Organisational structures, RACI matrices, and capability frameworks for AI-native operations
- **Change Management** — Communication playbooks, stakeholder engagement plans, and adoption measurement frameworks
- **AI Center of Excellence** — Design, staffing, and governance of a centralised AI coordination function

**Methodology:** Diagnose → Design → Pilot → Scale

## Pathway 04: AI Enablement

Production-grade AI deployment including AI agents, intelligent automation, and model operations.

**What You Get:**

- **AI Agent Development** — Custom autonomous agents for workflow automation, anomaly detection, and cross-system orchestration
- **Model Operations (MLOps)** — Training pipelines, model registries, monitoring dashboards, and automated retraining workflows
- **Governance & Risk Framework** — AI risk classification, bias auditing, explainability requirements, and regulatory compliance automation
- **Platform Integration** — Connect AI capabilities to existing enterprise systems through MCP connectors and API integrations

**Methodology:** Use Case Selection → Build & Train → Deploy & Monitor → Optimise

## Three Principles

1. **Business Outcomes First** — Every engagement starts with a measurable KPI and an accountable business owner
2. **Governance That Accelerates** — Enterprise controls mitigate risk through intelligent automation, not bureaucracy
3. **Architected for Repeatability** — Reusable patterns and knowledge handover so your teams own the result
