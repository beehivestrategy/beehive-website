What Conversational AI actually delivers

Not a chatbot, not another dashboard: a question-to-answer system wired to your governed data, with the evidence attached.

Answers in the channel

The assistant lives in WeChat, DingTalk, Feishu, Teams or your own portal. No new tab, no new login, no new habit to learn.

Every number is traceable

Each answer carries the query, the source table and the metric definition. When a figure is challenged, the evidence is already there.

Permissions travel with it

Role-based access is enforced at the data layer, not the UI. Two people can ask the same question and legitimately see different answers.

Scoped to one outcome first

We start with the single question your team chases most often, then widen coverage only after that answer is trusted.

EXAMPLES IN THE CHANNEL

Questions your team already asks

Four roles, four questions the assistant answers from your own data — with the source attached to every figure.

Retail · Store Ops

Which SKUs lost margin this week, and why?

Returns the three largest margin drops with the driver — promo depth, COGS shift or mix — each linked to the source table so the buyer can act today.

Behind it: daily margin fact · SKU dimension · gross_margin_pct

Finance

What is our net cash position across entities for the next 14 days?

Returns position by entity with opening balance, inflows and outflows shown, so the treasury lead sees the shortfall before it lands.

Behind it: treasury feed · entity mapping · cash_flow_vw

Supply Chain

Where are we at risk of stockout this week?

Returns at-risk SKUs per store with days-of-cover and supplier lead time, so the planner reorders the right lines first.

Behind it: inventory snapshot · supplier lead times · dos_forecast

Executive

Summarise this quarter’s AI readiness gaps.

Returns the ranked gap list from your last readiness audit with the owner and the recommended fix, so the board conversation starts from evidence.

Behind it: readiness audit · scorecard · gap_register

What sits underneath

Digital and data transformation still happen on every engagement — as enablers, scoped to the AI use case, never as the headline deliverable.
HOW IT WORKS

From a question to a sourced answer

Four steps sit between the question your team asks and an answer they can trust. Every step is visible — nothing happens inside a black box.

Step 1

Ask

The question arrives in the channel your team already opens — WeChat, DingTalk, Feishu, Teams or your portal. No new tool, no new login.

→
Step 2

Ground

The assistant resolves the question against your governed semantic layer — the same metric definitions your reports already use, so the number means what everyone expects.

→
Step 3

Govern

Role-based permissions, row-level access and audit logging are applied before any figure is returned. Two people can ask the same question and legitimately see different answers.

→
Step 4

Answer

The reply arrives with the query, source table and metric definition attached — so it can be challenged, traced and trusted, not just read.

Connects to SnowflakeBigQueryPostgreSQLSalesforceyour warehouse & files through governed MCP connectors — no open-ended credentials.

Two weeks from scope to handover
