ONE METHOD

One method, four starting points

Every scenario below starts with a question someone needs answered inside the tools they already use. Data work and integration are scoped backwards from that question. Then the same four-step delivery runs, whatever the sector.

01 Diagnose Agree the question
02 Scope Fix data access
03 Build Governed first release
04 Hand over Your team owns it

The same sequence runs in every situation below.

IN EVERY ENGAGEMENT

What you receive in every engagement

FOUR SITUATIONS

Four situations, four deliverable sets

Each card describes the work and the deliverable. There are no percentages on this page because we have none that are cleared for publication.

SITUATION 1 OF 4

Questions across stores, channels and stock

A commercial lead asks one question and currently waits for three different exports to be reconciled by hand.
BEFORE DAY ONE · What must be true first

Someone who owns the data definitions, and one report you can use as the starting reference.

FROM QUESTION TO HANDOVER
01

Starting point

A recurring question with a known owner — for example, why a category moved in a given week.

02

What gets built

A governed question-to-answer workflow over the sources that already exist, with role-based access and sources shown alongside the answer.

03

What you receive

A working assistant inside the team's existing channel, a documented set of data actions, and the checks that keep answers reproducible.

SITUATION 2 OF 4

Shift and maintenance questions on the floor

Supervisors need an answer during a shift, not a dashboard rebuilt by an analyst the following week.
BEFORE DAY ONE · What must be true first

Access to the relevant historians or tables, and agreement on who may see what.

FROM QUESTION TO HANDOVER
01

Starting point

A short list of questions that recur at handover, stoppage or quality review.

02

What gets built

Those questions mapped to governed data actions, with a review trail so an answer can be traced back to its source.

03

What you receive

A scoped first release, an audit trail, and handover documentation your own team can operate.

  • A working assistant
  • Documented answers
  • Ownership, in writing
SITUATION 3 OF 4

Reporting and control questions under review

A control function needs the answer and the evidence together, with a record of how the answer was produced.
BEFORE DAY ONE · What must be true first

A named data owner and a clear rule on what the assistant may and may not answer.

FROM QUESTION TO HANDOVER
01

Starting point

One reporting or control question where the current process is manual and repeatable.

02

What gets built

A conversational layer over approved sources, with access boundaries, logging and a scheduled rule review.

03

What you receive

The workflow, its governance rules, and the monitoring loop that flags drift after launch.

SAME DELIVERABLE SET, EVERY SITUATION
  • A working assistant

  • Ownership, in writing

Project and utilisation questions across teams

Partners ask similar questions every week, and each answer is assembled from a different spreadsheet.
BEFORE DAY ONE · What must be true first

A decision on the single source of truth, and someone available for a short diagnostic.

FROM QUESTION TO HANDOVER
01

Starting point

The three to five questions that actually drive a weekly decision.

02

What gets built

One governed assistant with those questions encoded as reusable data actions, delivered inside the existing collaboration tool.

03

What you receive

A first release in two weeks, enablement for the team, and full ownership transfer at handover.
